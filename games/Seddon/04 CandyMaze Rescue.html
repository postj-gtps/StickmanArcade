Stickman: Candyland Rescue

⭐ LEVEL 1
🗝️ KEY: NEEDED
🦷 DENTIST: SLEEPING
💥 BOULDER: 3/3 HP

🍭 CANDYLAND RESCUE TUTORIAL 🍬

🎮 STICKMAN CONTROLS

W A S D Move Stickman through the candy paths. (Arrow keys disabled!)

SPACEBAR Swing Pickaxe to smash obstacles.

🎯 YOUR MISSION

1. 🗝️ Find the Golden Key hidden randomly on a candy path.

2. ⛩️ Walk to the Candy Gate to unlock it with your key.

3. 🔒 Enter the gate — it closes & locks behind you creating a Safe Zone!

4. 💥 Use SPACEBAR to smash the Bubblegum Boulder (3 HP).

5. ♥ Walk to Timmy the Gnome to rescue him and beat the level!

⚠️ ENEMIES & HAZARDS

🦷 Angry Dentist: Awakens 5s after you move. He CANNOT pass through the Candy Gate, keeping you safe inside!

⚡ Cotton Candy Storm: Stepping on the trap tile unleashes a gentle gumdrop rain. Avoid getting hit!

WASD Move • SPACEBAR Pickaxe • 🗝️ Find Key • ⛩️ Unlock Gate • 🔒 Safe Zone Locks Behind You!
